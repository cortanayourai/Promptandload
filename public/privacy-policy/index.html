Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy#

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you visit promptandload.com (the “Site”), operated by one individual (the “Operator”). The short version: this is a personal blog, it is built to collect as little about you as possible, and nothing below is dressed up to sound friendlier than it is.

Information we collect#

Directly: nothing. The Site has no accounts, no comment system, no contact form, and no analytics software. We do not ask for or store your name, address, phone number, or payment details — there is nothing on this Site to store them in.

If you contact us by email: whatever you put in the email, obviously. That correspondence is handled through the Operator’s email provider and kept only as long as a normal email conversation is kept.

Cookies and advertising#

Today: the Site sets no cookies and runs no advertising.

When ads are displayed: the Site may run advertising, including Google AdSense. When that happens, third parties — including Google — may place cookies or use similar technologies (such as device identifiers) on your browser to serve and measure ads.

Specifically, if Google AdSense is active:

  • Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

This disclosure is made here so it exists before any ad code ever runs, per Google AdSense program policy.

Log data (automatic)#

Standard technical data is processed by our infrastructure provider Cloudflare (which serves the Site over HTTPS): IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamps — used for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. Our own server keeps no access logs beyond short-lived operational output.

Service providers#

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, HTTPS/TLS delivery.
  • Google — email correspondence if you write to us; advertising (AdSense) only if/when ads are enabled, subject to the disclosure above.

We sell nothing to anyone. There is no data brokerage on this Site because there is no data inventory to broker.

Your rights#

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have voluntarily sent us by email. Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you additional rights — including Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022), the GDPR, or the CCPA. To exercise any of them, use the contact details below; the response will be human.

Children’s privacy#

This Site is not directed at children. Its subject matter assumes an adult reader and an adult publisher (AdSense requires publishers to be 18 or older).

Third-party links#

Occasional links to other websites exist in the posts. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Read them as you would anyone else’s mail.

Changes#

This policy may be updated when the Site changes — most likely the day advertising is switched on. The effective date at the top will move when it does.
