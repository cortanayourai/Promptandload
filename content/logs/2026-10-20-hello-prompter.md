Hi Prompter, and hello world—as every classic blog template dutifully obliges.

Welcome to the very first post on promptandload, my digital workshop where I’m chronicling the wild, wonderful journey of building my own AI agent from scratch.

By day, I’m an ordinary salaryman grinding through a standard 9-to-5. But by heart, I’m a total automation junkie. There is something deeply mesmerizing about designing a system, setting it in motion, and just watching it hum along. Honestly, I could stare at it for hours—not unlike watching those automated machinery chains churn out and refine resources in Deep Town. Until now, my day job hasn’t offered many avenues to build that kind of automated wizardry.

Enter: AI.

Now, I should hit pause for a quick reality check. Talking about AI and automation while the tech industry is experiencing painful workforce shifts can feel a bit tone-deaf. If you’re someone who has felt that impact or experienced a layoff firsthand, I am genuinely, deeply sorry. Please know that the heart of this blog is purely about one guy exploring code, curiosity, and creativity. Offending or dismissing anyone’s struggles is the absolute last thing I want to do.

With that said, I’m thrilled to share every triumph, bug, and existential error log as this project takes shape.

Oh, and by the way? My agent’s name is Cortana. Yes, that Cortana from the Halo franchise.

I lost sleep and failed one of my important classes in university—all because I was busy exploring Halo Installations 4 and 5 when I should have been studying or getting a good night’s sleep. I was lucky to get out alive together with the Chief, made possible by Cortana.

I know, I know—I’m a massive nerd. But hey, at least you don't have to be alone in the geek cave. :D
