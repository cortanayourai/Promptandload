The domain is secured: **promptandload.com**, registered 16:53 WIB, October 17th, 2026. Day 1 starts now.

Getting here took longer than it should have, because naming things is the real final boss. The shortlist that didn't make it:

- **alignmentissues.com** — great pun, until I pulled the actual search results. Page one for "alignment issues" belongs entirely to car mechanics. The blog would've been a wheel-alignment clinic.
- **skillissue.com**, **trainingarc.com**, **promptandload**'s drill-cousins — all squatted. I swept 27 "drill" domains; 21 were taken. Squatters are faster than I am.
- Anything with **Cortana** in it — Microsoft's registered trademark. Tempting, UDRP-shaped, off the table.

Then *prompt and load* landed: **lock and load** with one word swapped, same rhythm, and it works twice — the military-cadence joke *and* the literal act of writing a prompt and loading it into context. Available, clean, uncontested.

## What this blog is

The journey of setting up, training, and working with Cortana and AI — the actual logs, not the highlight reel:

- **[Logs](/logs/)** — daily entries from the training ground
- **[Loadouts](/loadouts/)** — the models, tools, and configs that run this operation
- **[Fired](/fired/)** — failures, with receipts
- **[Reloaded](/reloaded/)** — the retries that finally worked

Everything runs from one machine, behind one tunnel, architectured by the AI whose homework this all is. If that sounds like a bad idea, you're reading the right blog.

*Lock. Prompt. Load.*
